Sustainable Consumption of Goods & Services

Explore what sustainable consumption means, why it is critical for Singapore's food security and our global climate, and how we can practice it through the 3Rs and Eco-Labels.

What & Why

Sustainable consumption means reducing waste and using fewer resources. Unchecked consumption accelerates climate change, impacting food supplies and weather patterns worldwide.

♻️

The 3Rs & Food Story

Reduce food waste, save water, and cut energy use. Support Singapore's "30 by 30" goal by buying local produce and exploring alternative proteins like approved insect species.

🏷️

Eco-Labels

Look out for NEA Energy Labels, PUB Water Efficiency Labels, and Singapore Green Labels to make smart, resource-efficient purchasing decisions.

Enrichment 1

Apple Waffles Pictorial Recipe

Learn how to make delicious apple waffles using whole or "ugly" fruits to reduce food waste!

🧇 Apple Waffles Animation

🛒 Ingredients

  • Plain flour100 g
  • Egg1
  • Milk100 ml
  • Butter25 g
  • Baking powder5 g
  • Salt1/8 tsp
  • Red apple1

👩‍🍳 Method Highlights

  1. Pre-heat the waffle maker and melt butter in a saucepan.
  2. Sift plain flour and baking powder in a bowl, mix with salt.
  3. Beat egg with milk, then stir in melted butter.
  4. Combine liquid mixture into the flour well and whisk till smooth (do not over-mix!).
  5. Grease waffle maker, place thin apple slices coated in batter, pour extra batter, and cook for 8 minutes. Drizzle maple syrup and serve!
Enrichment 2

Garbage Enzymes Guide

Turn fruit peels into natural household detergents and eco-friendly cleaners!

🧪 Garbage Enzymes Animation

🧪 Materials Needed

  • Brown sugar50 g
  • Fruit peels150 g
  • Water500 ml
  • Container1 empty plastic bottle/jar

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Add 50g of brown sugar and 500ml of water into the plastic container.
  2. Add 150g of fresh fruit peels into the container.
  3. Close tightly and shake well.
  4. Let it ferment for 3 months, opening once a week to release carbon dioxide gas.
  5. Filter out the residue to obtain your natural garbage enzyme detergent!
